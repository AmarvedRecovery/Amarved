Our Approach

The Recovery Framework

We do not promise quick fixes or medical cures. Instead, we offer a systematic, evidence-informed framework for expanding your energy envelope.

1. Pacing Mastery

Learn to accurately read your body's signals to avoid the push-and-crash cycle. Establish a safe baseline for daily activities.

2. Brain Retraining

Utilize neuroplasticity principles to help your nervous system shift out of chronic 'fight or flight' (sympathetic dominance).

3. Somatic Support

Gentle, restorative techniques to rebuild trust with your physical body through trauma-informed somatic experiencing.
