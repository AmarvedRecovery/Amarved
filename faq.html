Frequently Asked Questions

Is this program a substitute for medical treatment?

No. The Amarved Recovery System provides educational and coaching services. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We do not prescribe medications or offer medical diagnoses.

Do you guarantee a cure?

No ethical practitioner guarantees a cure for chronic illnesses like ME/CFS or Long COVID. What we provide is a science-backed framework to maximize your body's healing potential, significantly expand your energy envelope, and improve your quality of life.

Will I have to do graded exercise therapy (GET)?

Absolutely not. We strictly oppose GET, as pushing through Post-Exertional Malaise (PEM) is actively harmful. Our approach relies on finding your absolute safe baseline and using microscopic exposures only when your nervous system is fully regulated and safe.

How much time does the program require daily?

The program is designed for people with very limited energy. Formal exercises take about 15-20 minutes a day. The rest of the program is about modifying how you go about your existing daily activities.
