The Process

How It Works

We take a structured, non-dogmatic approach to expanding your energy envelope. No quick fixes, just consistent, science-informed nervous system regulation.

1

Discovery Call

We begin with a free 30-minute consultation. We listen to your story, assess where you are in your journey, and determine if the Amarved framework is safe and appropriate for your current baseline.

2

Baseline Assessment

During the first week, we conduct a deep dive into your energy patterns. We identify your absolute baseline—the level of activity you can sustain without triggering Post-Exertional Malaise (PEM) or symptom flares.

3

Somatic Safety & Pacing

We implement aggressive resting and structural pacing. Concurrently, you learn somatic tracking techniques to shift your autonomic nervous system from sympathetic dominance (fight/flight) to parasympathetic rest.

4

Cognitive Retraining

We introduce neuroplasticity exercises to rewire the limbic system. This helps break the brain's habit of interpreting normal bodily sensations or minor stressors as severe threats, reducing the symptom loop.

5

Gradual Expansion

Once your baseline is stable for an extended period, we use micro-exposures to very gradually expand your energy envelope. We monitor closely to ensure safety and prevent setbacks.
