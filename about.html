Our Story

Compassion rooted in experience.

We understand the isolating nature of invisible illnesses because we've been there. Amarved was built to provide the structural support we wish we had during our darkest days.

The Founder's Journey

After a suffering from chronic stress for decades, our founder, Amar, developed what was later diagnosed as severe ME/CFS and Fibromyalgia. Doctors offered little hope, and conventional treatments repeatedly failed.

Through years of obsessive research into neuroscience, somatic pacing, autonomic nervous and attending several international programs, a personal framework was born. What started as a desperate attempt to regain basic function slowly evolved into a comprehensive system that guided Amar back to a thriving life.

Amarved Recovery System was founded so no one else has to figure this out alone.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, evidence-informed, and deeply compassionate coaching for adults navigating chronic, energy-limiting illnesses. We aim to replace desperation with structured hope.

Our Vision

A world where individuals with invisible illnesses are validated, understood, and equipped with the tools necessary to expand their energy envelopes and reclaim their quality of life.
