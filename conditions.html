Conditions We Support

While the labels differ, the underlying mechanisms of autonomic dysfunction and nervous system dysregulation are deeply connected. Our approach targets these root physiological patterns.

ME/CFS

Myalgic Encephalomyelitis / Chronic Fatigue Syndrome

  • Post-Exertional Malaise (PEM)
  • Unrefreshing Sleep
  • Orthostatic Intolerance

Long COVID

Post-Acute Sequelae of SARS-CoV-2 infection (PASC)

  • Severe Fatigue & Brain Fog
  • Dysautonomia / POTS
  • Breathlessness & Palpitations

Fibromyalgia

Central Sensitization Syndrome

  • Widespread Chronic Pain
  • Extreme Fatigue
  • Cognitive Difficulties

IBS

Irritable Bowel Syndrome & Gut-Brain Axis

  • Gut Hypersensitivity
  • Chronic Digestion Issues
  • Stress-Triggered Flares

Severe Burnout

HPA Axis Dysfunction

  • Total Exhaustion
  • Emotional Blunting
  • Inability to Recover via Rest

Brain Fog

Cognitive Dysfunction

  • Poor Memory Recall
  • Lack of Concentration
  • Sensory Overload

Chronic Pain

Central Sensitization & Discomfort

  • Muscle & Joint Stiffness
  • Hyperalgesia (Increased Sensitivity)
  • Persistent Physical Discomfort

Chronic Anxiety

Nervous System Hyperarousal

  • Overactive Flight-or-Fight response
  • Somatic Panic Symptoms
  • Persistent Heart Rate Spikes

Chronic Depression

Autonomic Downregulation & Low Energy

  • Loss of Pleasure & Motivation
  • Neurotransmitter Depletion
  • Severe Persistent Fatigue
