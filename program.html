The 12-Week Coaching Program

A structured, compassionate approach to regaining control over your health. By combining private coaching, educational modules, and somatic exercises, we provide a holistic toolkit for recovery.

Weekly 1:1 Sessions

Personalized 60-minute video calls to analyze your week, adjust pacing limits, and introduce new nervous system regulation tools.

Extensive Resources

Community Access

Join a private, highly moderated support group of peers who actually understand what you are going through.

Curriculum Overview

Weeks 1-3: Assessment & Stabilization

Establishing an absolute baseline, implementing radical rest, and learning to track somatic signals without judgment.

Weeks 4-6: Autonomic Regulation

Introducing targeted breathing protocols, vagus nerve stimulation techniques, and identifying micro-stressors that drain your battery.

Weeks 7-9: Cognitive Repatterning

Addressing the psychological toll of chronic illness. Learning to decouple symptoms from danger signals in the limbic system.

Weeks 10-12: Expansion & Maintenance

Applying micro-exposures to carefully expand the energy envelope. Developing a long-term maintenance plan for continued recovery post-program.
